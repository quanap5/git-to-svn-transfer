# Direction: readme-banner
Concept: One continuous rail runs through the whole piece: Git commits enter on the left, are bracketed into three numbered blocks, pass a wall of green checks, stop at a gate where a person carries each block across by hand, and land as SVN revisions on the right.
Base: developer-workflow · energy 3 · juice budget: soft overshoot (`back.out(1.4)`), smears on UI, one soft impact per scene on its key word (`slam(el, t, { soft: true })` with no blur, flash without shake); no shake, no elastic, no squash.
Locks: palette bg `#10131a` · ink `#e8ecef` · mute `#808891` · surface `#171b24` · border `#2f3540` · accent `#4a74f0` (groups, focus, rail pulse) · add `#3ab86e` (checks and `ready` only) · del `#ec6040` (the two struck commands only) · merge `#b46ee8` (the human SVN commit and the finale only) · warn unused; all fills flat · type Inter 800 (cover headline), 700 (scene headlines), 600 (sublines); JetBrains Mono Bold for every label and command, Regular only at 48 px or larger. Master-size floors, so the GIF keeps the profile's 22 px legibility: mono 44 px, Inter 60 px, strokes 6 px, dots 32 px.
Motif: The rail (8 px horizontal line) and the group block (rounded 6 px surface chip with a 3 px accent border and a mono number). The blocks ride the rail one at a time in s01, are cut out of the commit dots in s02, stack cumulatively in s03, cross the gate and turn into purple revision dots in s04, and sit paired one-to-one with those dots in s05. A small accent pulse rides the rail left to right during every hold.
Rhythm: s01 calm · s02 build · s03 build (stepped cascade) · s04 peak · s05 breathe
Taste rules:
- Never use blur, glow, radial gradients or soft shadows: flat fills and hard edges only, so the 960 px, 15 fps GIF stays clean. One clip-path at most, and only if a line mask cannot be done with `overflow: hidden`.
- Never move the whole frame during a hold (no `settleCam`, no background drift). Hold motion is local: the rail pulse, a caret blink, a check drawing.
- Never use green, red or purple decoratively. Green means a check passed, red means a command the tool does not run, purple means a person committed to SVN.
- Never show a logo, wordmark, avatar initials or a mascot. The person is a plain ink circle labelled `you`.
- Never put more than one headline and one label cluster in motion at once, and never animate behind a line while it is being read.
- Never show a label that is not in the facts table. Counts of commit dots are unlabelled, and only r101, r102 and r103 appear as numbers.

Controller decisions on the director's concerns:
- Local hold motion instead of `settleCam` or background drift is accepted.
- No captions, no signature, no audio element, no opening fade from black: frame 0 already shows the background and the origin dot, so a static preview of the GIF is not empty.
- The flat dot grid is optional: remove it if it dithers in the GIF.
- If s04 is too dense on the contact sheet, cut the two file rows first, then the step chips.

## s01-cover · cover · calm

- Focal beats: "Git to SVN" → the headline, top left. "One" → the first block leaving the origin dot. "at a time" → the third block following alone, with the first two already parked on the right.
- Hero moment: Masked line reveal on "Git to SVN": the three words rise from below the mask with a 4 degree rotation that resolves.
- Entrances: origin dot → pops, `back.out(1.4)`, first element on screen (it is the loop seam). Rail → draws left to right from the dot, `expo.out`, starting as the dot settles. Headline → `lineIn`, `expo.out`, starting when the rail is about 40% drawn. Subline → rises from its own mask, `power4.out`, starting when "SVN" is about half settled; "One" turns accent with the kinetic-keyword scale 1 → 1.12 → 1. Blocks → smear in from the dot one after another (`skewX` resolving to 0), `expo.out`, with clearly unequal gaps so it reads as "one at a time".
- Depth: bg flat `#10131a` with a static coarse dot grid (4 px dots, 64 px pitch, `#171b24`, no opacity ramp) / mid headline and subline / fg rail and blocks. Hold motion: the accent pulse rides the rail.
- Exit: Branch-off. The headline and subline exit upward into their masks (`power3.in`, about 60% of the entrance time), while the rail slides down to y 640 and the three blocks stay on it as the cut-points of s02.
- Avoid: an arrow glyph between "Git" and "SVN"; centring the headline; numbering the blocks before s02; fading the rail out between scenes.
